⚠️ Key Concerns — Detail
1. Unauthorized Access
Unregistered brokers, agents, and visitors freely entering premises without verification. Gate logs show gaps in recording visitor details, creating a security risk for all 160 households.
2. Property Damage
Lift damage during heavy shifting and parking violations leading to vehicle damage. The cost of repairs is being shared by all residents even when caused by a single individual — addressed in R-01.
AGM ACTION REQUIRED
Both concerns are addressed through Resolutions R-01 and R-02 on today's agenda.
🛡️ Three Priority Pillars
1
Security
Digital visitor management, CCTV upgrade, agent registration, and flat-level access controls to prevent unauthorized entry and protect all residents.
2
Financial Discipline
Enforce timely maintenance payments, levy penalties on defaulters, and maintain a transparent budget. ₹37L is already secured in Fixed Deposit.
3
Governance
Formal policies for damage liability, penalty schedules, and resident compliance — ensuring every resident is treated fairly under the same rules.

⚖️ Resolutions — Full Text
R-01 · CRITICALProperty Damage & Penalty Policy
Resolved that MST Society formally adopts the Property Damage Liability Policy including penalty schedule for lift damage (min. ₹5,000), structural damage (min. ₹25,000), and mandatory shifting deposits of ₹5,000.
R-02 · HIGHEnhanced Security Measures
Resolved to implement digital visitor management and mandatory broker/agent registration effective 1 August 2026, with full compliance from all flat owners within 30 days.
R-03 · HIGHAnnual Budget FY 2026-27
Resolved to approve the proposed budget of ₹70 Lakhs for FY 2026-27 (7.7% increase), covering enhanced security, CCTV upgrade, mobile app implementation, and routine maintenance.
R-04 · HIGHDefaulter Enforcement Protocol
Resolved to authorise the Managing Committee to issue legal notices and initiate recovery proceedings against 12 identified high-value defaulters with outstanding dues exceeding ₹50,000.
